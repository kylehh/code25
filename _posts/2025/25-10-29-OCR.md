## 1 Combine Tokens
To combine text and image tokens, CLIP is a key work from OpenAI
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-10-29-OCR_files/clip.png)
and this lays fundation of multimodal of working w any other modalities. Here is [NExT-GPT paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.05519) about it
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-10-29-OCR_files/nextgpt.png)
and there are multiple ways to combine text and image/video tokens, like linear(LLava), attention(Qwen-VL) and cross-attention(Q-former, BLIP-2) (From Haoran's previous [Vary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.06109))
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-10-29-OCR_files/combine.png)
Another example from [DeepSeek-VL2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.10302), which uses **Dynamic Tiling**. 
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-10-29-OCR_files/dsvl2.png)
This tiling idea is coming from [InternVL 1.5 paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.16821)
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-10-29-OCR_files/intern.png)

## 2 Multiple Image Encoders
Due to the complicated situation of various images due to size, ratio and resolutions, we may employee multiple image encoders, like [DeepSeek-VL paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.05525) uses 2 SAM-B and SigLIP-L.
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-10-29-OCR_files/dsvl.png)
and [Cambrian-1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.16860) from LeCun's team in NYU even push to 4 encoders, SigLIP+DINOv2+ConvNext+CLIP
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-10-29-OCR_files/cambrian.png)
[Patch-Pack NaViT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.06304) from Google proposed a way to use encoder for any ratio and resolution -- *Multiple patches from different images are packed in a single sequence—termed Patch n’ Pack—which enables variable resolution while preserving the aspect ratio*. The downside of this method is generating too many tokens
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-10-29-OCR_files/navit.png)
