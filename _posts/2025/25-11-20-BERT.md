# 1 BERT Pre-Training
Two phases for the Pretraining phase.
1. Masked LM: Fill in the blanks
2. Next Setence Prediction: If two sentences are related
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-11-20-BERT_files/pretrain1.png)
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-11-20-BERT_files/pretrain2.png)
The input is sum of 3 embeddings
1. Token embedding is from **wordpiece** w 30k tokens
2. Segement embedding is A or B
3. Position embedding
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-11-20-BERT_files/sum.png)
The output of each word is mapped to 30k neurons (token vocab size) and compaire w one hot encoding for a loss calculation.  
![Alt text](/code25/assets/images/2025/25-11-20-BERT_files/pretrain3.png)
